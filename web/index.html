How to Use

Click Start Camera and allow camera access. Keep your complete hand visible and show one sign at a time. After a sign is accepted, briefly remove your hand before repeating it.

Live Recognition

The browser uses MediaPipe Hands to extract hand landmarks. A 30-frame landmark sequence is sent through WebSocket to the TensorFlow V4 model for prediction.
